ABETWORKS WORKCRM SAAS PLATFORM DEVELOPMENT PLAN
=================================================

PROJECT OVERVIEW
---------------
Application Type: Multi-Tenant CRM SaaS Platform - ABETWORKS WORKCRM
Target Architecture: Node.js Backend with Multi-Tenant Architecture
Deployment: Scalable Cloud Solution
Purpose: CRM-as-a-Service for multiple companies under ABETWORKS brand

BUSINESS MODEL
-------------
- Super Admin Panel to manage all tenant companies under ABETWORKS WORKCRM
- Each company gets their own isolated CRM instance branded as ABETWORKS WORKCRM
- Subscription-based pricing model managed by ABETWORKS
- Companies pay to use ABETWORKS WORKCRM for their business operations
- White-label capabilities for resellers under ABETWORKS brand

BRANDING STRATEGY
---------------
- ABETWORKS as the parent company brand
- WORKCRM as the product brand
- Each company gets their own subdomain: companyname.workcrm.abetworks.com
- Customizable themes within ABETWORKS WORKCRM platform
- Professional onboarding experience with ABETWORKS branding

TECHNOLOGY STACK
---------------
Backend: Node.js with Express.js
Database: PostgreSQL (Multi-tenant with row-level security)
Frontend: React.js with TypeScript
Authentication: JWT with refresh tokens
Authorization: Role-based access control (RBAC)
Payment Processing: Stripe integration
File Storage: AWS S3 or Cloudinary
Email Service: SendGrid or AWS SES
Caching: Redis
Real-time: Socket.IO
Containerization: Docker
Orchestration: Kubernetes (optional)
Monitoring: Prometheus + Grafafana or New Relic
